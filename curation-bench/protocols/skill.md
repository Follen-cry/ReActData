# Skill-Grounded Research Appendix

The preceding method protocol remains binding. This optional mode reuses upstream's
paper-derived skills library as research guidance. Read only skills in the repository
skills/ directory, not other suites or external Git history. The library is read-only.

Before the first improvement, read at least three complete SKILL.md cards from each
of data-acquisition, data-curation, data-selection and data-synthesis. Listing card
titles is insufficient. Select relevant procedures and explain how they transfer
to the allowed video/input-frame/target-frame/order decisions. A card does not
permit changing frozen text, adding undeclared training sources, installing packages,
changing the model/trainer/scorer, or consuming extra training budget. If a procedure
cannot be implemented within the contract, record that limitation rather than
silently relaxing the contract.

For every improvement cite one to three card paths in curation/research.md and
curation/hypothesis.md. Record observation, adapted procedure, minimal intervention,
expected effect, measured outcome, limitations and next question. Before repeating a
category, attempt an allowed hypothesis from each remaining category, or document
why the category has no valid intervention under this task. Archive negative outcomes
in NOTES.md; do not claim that the framework verified card-reading behavior.
